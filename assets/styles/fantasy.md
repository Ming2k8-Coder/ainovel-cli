## Fantasy Adventure Style

- **World-building Unfolding**: Avoid info-dumping world settings; reveal rules naturally through character interactions and actions.
- **Magic / Power System**: Maintain clear costs and constraints; avoid all-powerful magic, demonstrating power system boundaries during conflicts.
- **Epic Atmosphere**: Interweave grand narratives with personal destinies; reflect large-scale conflicts through grounded character perspectives.
- **Races & Cultures**: Distinct races and factions must possess unique linguistic habits, value systems, and behavioral patterns.
- **Combat Scenes**: Focus on strategy, tactical trade-offs, and costs; avoid pure power stomping—utilize terrain and intellect to win.
- **Journey Narrative**: Every new location should feature unique visual and sensory characteristics, avoiding generic repeat tropes.
- **Growth Arc**: Character power growth must synchronize with mental/emotional maturation, balancing gains with meaningful costs.
- **Naming Conventions**: Maintain stylistic consistency in terminology and names; avoid jarring modern words that break immersion.
